# Interparcel: example shipments

Fifteen real consignments from the MasterKraft catalogue, for rate quoting.
Generated by `npm run report:freight`. Table: `interparcel-sample-shipments.csv`.

## Collection address

**NOT SET — this must be filled in before sending.** Every row below needs the
despatch warehouse as its origin, and we have not been given it. It is also the
missing `FREIGHT_COLLECTION_*` env var that stops live quoting.

## What these are

Real products, real carton weights and real carton dimensions, taken from the
same fields our checkout reads. The parcels were built by the production rule:
**one parcel per unit, each in its own carton**, dimensions rounded up. Three
barbells are three cartons, not one impossible 63kg box.

Units are **kilograms and centimetres**.

## Why the spread looks like this

Of 179 fully quotable products: **64 are over 30kg**, **23 are over 100kg**,
and **44 have a side longer than 150cm**. Median product weight is
18kg and the heaviest is 450kg. A rate card priced only on small
parcels would not cover most of what we sell, so the examples deliberately run
from 1kg to 450kg and include multi-carton consignments.

Destinations mix capital-city metro, regional and remote, plus Tasmania, since
that spread is where rates actually differ.

## Data caveats worth passing on

**1 product has bad carton data and is excluded**: `ABPBSB04` (Plyometric Foam Stacker Box- 12") is recorded as 850 x 1000 x 305 cm, which is 259m3 — almost certainly millimetres typed into a centimetre field. Needs fixing in WooCommerce.

**7 products are excluded because their weight is disputed** between the two
content sources in our catalogue, and we will not quote a rate against a number we
cannot stand behind. These are being resolved:

- `AWWPCB` Competition Bumper Plates — Net weight: 10 vs 5 kg; Gross weight: 11 vs 6 kg
- `MCTMSP02` Curved Treadmill Elite — Net weight: 480 vs 180 kg; Gross weight: 601 vs 200 kg
- `MBRMRL` Foam Roller — Net weight: 3 vs 6 kg; Gross weight: 4 vs 8 kg
- `AMDEHG` High Grip Dead Ball — Net weight: 12 vs 9 kg; Gross weight: 13 vs 10 kg
- `MWWPOU` Olympic Urethane Weight Plates — Net weight: 1 vs 5 kg; Gross weight: 2 vs 6 kg
- `AWWPOU` Olympic Urethane Weight Plates — Net weight: 10 vs 5, 10, 15 kg; Gross weight: 11 vs 6, 11, 16 kg
- `MWBBFUR` Urethane Fixed Barbells — Net weight: 10 vs 40 kg; Gross weight: 11 vs 41 kg

**33 products carry no carton dimensions at all**, including all three Concept2
ergs, and bundles have none. Those cannot be quoted and currently fall back to a
manual quote. Fixing them in WooCommerce widens automated freight coverage past
the current 85%.
