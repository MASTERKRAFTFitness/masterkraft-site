# Google Business Profile copy — MasterKraft

**Status: DRAFTED HERE, NOT YET APPLIED.** Google Business Profile (GBP) can
only be edited by a signed-in owner or manager, either at
business.google.com or by searching "MasterKraft" on Google while signed in.
Paste each block into the matching field. The character counts are checked
against GBP's limits.

Every fact is taken from the live site, as in `docs/seo-gyms1-listing-copy.md`.
That means no prices, no invented figures, no STRONG reference, "fitout" as one
word and Australian English (`docs/opinly-brand-voice.md`).

GBP is the single highest-value listing for local search. It drives the map
pack for searches like "gym equipment Melbourne" and "gym fitout near me", so
it is worth doing properly.

## 0. Storefront: confirmed

**Confirmed 4 Oct: customers visit the Thomastown premises and staff are there.**
So set it up as a **storefront**: show the address, set opening hours, and

storefront by video or postcard to the address.

## 1. Business name

> MasterKraft

**Only the real trading name.** Don't add keywords ("MasterKraft Gym Equipment
Melbourne"). It breaks Google's guidelines and is one of the most common
reasons a profile gets suspended. The keywords go in categories, services and
the description instead.

## 2. Categories

GBP categories come from a fixed list, so type each one and pick the exact
match from the dropdown. **The primary category matters more for ranking than
anything else on the profile.**

| | Category | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary** | **Exercise equipment store** | Closest match to "gym equipment" searches, and right for a storefront customers visit. |
| Secondary | Fitness equipment wholesaler | Trade / wholesale supply |
| Secondary | Sporting goods wholesaler | Broader equipment searches |
| Secondary | Flooring store *(or "Rubber products supplier")* | Commercial gym flooring |
| Secondary | Interior fitting contractor *(or "Contractor")* | Covers the fitout / installation side |

Don't use "Gym" or "Fitness centre". MasterKraft doesn't sell memberships, and
the wrong category gets the listing in front of the wrong searchers.

## 10. Photos

GBP profiles with plenty of recent photos get noticeably more clicks.

- **Logo:** square, from `public/brand/`.
- **Cover:** a wide shot of a finished fitout (Fernwood Pakenham or REVL
  Collingwood).
- **At work / team:** installs in progress, the team on site.
- **Exterior + interior:** only if it is a storefront (helps Google verify the
  location).
- **Projects:** 10+ real fitout photos, then **add a few every month**. Fresh
  uploads are an activity signal.

## 12. Reviews — the biggest ranking lever after category

- From the GBP dashboard, copy the **"Ask for reviews"** short link.
- Send it to recent fitout clients, franchise partners and trade buyers. Ask
  them to mention the type of fitout and the suburb. Keywords in reviews help
  local ranking.
- **Reply to every review**, good or bad, within a few days.
- Never offer anything in return for reviews. Google removes them and can
  penalise the profile.

Suggested request message:
> Hi [Name], thanks again for trusting MasterKraft with your [gym/studio]
> fitout. If you have a minute, a short Google review would really help other
> gym owners find us: [review link]. Thanks!
