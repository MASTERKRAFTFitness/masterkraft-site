MasterKraft: commercial gym equipment and complete gym fitouts, engineered for fitness

MasterKraft is an Australian commercial gym equipment supplier and fitout company based in Thomastown, in Melbourne's north. We design, supply and install gym equipment for the people who run training floors for a living: independent gym owners, franchise groups, boutique and group-training studios, personal trainers, schools and universities, elite sports clubs, clinics and corporate facilities. We also supply commercial-grade equipment for home gyms.

To date we have fitted out 229 sites across 12 countries. Our Thomastown premises are open to customers Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

Commercial gym equipment

Our range covers everything a modern training floor needs:

- Strength equipment: pin-loaded and plate-loaded machines, multi-station units, benches and hyperextension benches.
- Rigs and racks: power racks, functional training rigs and lifting platforms.
- Weightlifting and free weights: barbells, plates, dumbbells, kettlebells and cable attachments.

- Gym storage: racks and storage that keep the floor clear and safe.
- Gym equipment packages: pre-specified bundles for common room sizes and training styles.

Everything we supply is chosen for one job: standing up to a busy floor of paying members, every day, for years.

How a MasterKraft fitout works

1. Scope: we survey your site and design a floor layout for your footprint, your members and the way you train.
2. Spec: we put together a standardised equipment schedule, in your colours and with your branding where you want it.
3. Supply: manufacture and freight are staged and sequenced to your opening date or rollout plan.
4. Support: we install, commission and induct your team, then stay accountable after handover.
