## Corrected

Five figures did not survive the day. Recorded because the reasons recur.

**1. The handover said to group by the range STEM.** `MWBBFUR` holds both the
Fixed PU Straight Barbell and the Fixed PU Curl Barbell, so stem-grouping reports
one product where there are two and sends back one photograph for both. The key
is brand plus the name before `" - "`. `ranges.ts` says this at more length and
is right.

**2. The first collapse returned 142 products where there are 92.** Apparel names
carry no `" - "` at all — they read `Sweatshirt (Unisex) (L)` — so splitting on
the separator reached none of the 52 Apparel codes. Stripping the trailing
garment size first turns 48 codes into 12 garments.

**3. That split was hand-rolled next to the rule it duplicates.**
`erp-catalogue` already exports `splitUnitName`, and its version knows a third
shape: `CONCEPT 2 - Ski Erg with PM5` is a BRAND before the separator, not a
range, and splitting it collapses four distinct ergs into one product called
"CONCEPT 2". Nothing in the gap set is brand-headed today, so the copy was right
by luck. Replaced in `cb9ea92`.

**4. "32 products hidden" was 18.** The report derived from `filterListable`,
which governs the clearance listing alone. Every one of its 32 rows rendered
$0.00 — which is what the "$9,273.92" headline was actually adding up.

**5. The Concept2 recommendation was wrong, and acting on it would have hurt.**
Three ergs were called "$2,770 and one measurement away". They are not hidden:
`concept-2-ski-erg-with-pm5` sells at $1,650 and
`concept-2-row-erg-with-standard-legs` at $1,705, while the suppressed `c2-`
pages are archive duplicates rendering $0.00. Measuring them would have
resurrected a $0.00 page beside each live one.

**The pattern in 4 and 5 is the one to carry forward:** a report over the frozen
snapshot answers a question about WooCommerce pages, not about products. Re-derive
from the ERP, which is the same warning the photography handover gives about
counting image gaps from snapshot page counts.
